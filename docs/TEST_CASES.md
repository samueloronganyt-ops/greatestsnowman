## Manual device check

1. Run the game using the README command and open the printed LAN URL on a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi.
2. Log in with a second account on another device, join the host room, ready both players, and start a match.
3. On desktop, verify WASD/arrow directions, drag look signs, Shift dash, Q/right-mouse aim, C/Ctrl crouch, crosshair, mouse charge, pause, and map collapse. On mobile, drag the left stick while dragging the right arena area; verify simultaneous movement/look and no collisions among Snowball, Aim, Crouch, and Dash.
4. Exercise stand → crouch → stand, dash in open space, dash into a wall, and wait out the cooldown. Throw from both stances. Verify the snowball appears at the hand on release and the cooldown ring fills back up.
5. Hold and release **SNOWBALL**; verify the charge meter advances and a charged throw occurs. Tap it for a normal throw. Check Aim and Crouch/Dash controls.
6. Rotate the phone between portrait and landscape, collect a pickup, then open Pause and choose Resume. Check for clipped controls or browser scrolling/zoom.
7. Sign into the same account on another window/device and confirm the inline takeover prompt replaces its game connection.
8. Use the browser's performance tools or a device FPS counter during a live match. Record device/browser and the approximate frame rate here when available.
9. Check that the Christmas Arena spawn pads, mirrored barrier rows, center island/statue and gifts, side ice stacks, inner corner trees, and lights appear where expected. Walk around cover for stuck gaps, then compare Low, Medium and High graphics.
10. On a fresh page, verify the Christmas title art and 3D snowman load before login. Move the pointer over the snowman and Play button, click the snowman, then Play. Confirm a valid saved session skips the login card, normal login never returns to title, and Log Out does. Check the image/art fallback by disabling WebGL support if available.
11. Tab through the title controls and activate them with Enter/Space. Confirm sound mute persists, fullscreen button works, the Quit dialog traps focus, Stay returns to the same screen, and Yes signs out/leaves the room before showing Thanks if the browser cannot close the tab. Test Play Again and reduced-motion preference at desktop and 360x640 portrait/landscape sizes.

No physical-phone FPS measurement is claimed by the automated results.

HOW TO PLAY

PC — Move: WASD / arrow keys. Look: drag the arena. Aim: hold right mouse or press Q. Throw: hold/release Space or left mouse. Crouch: C or Ctrl (hold by default; change to toggle in Pause settings). Dash: Shift. Pause: Esc.

Mobile — Move: left joystick. Look: drag the open arena. Aim: AIM. Throw: hold/release SNOWBALL. Crouch and Dash: use their buttons. Pause: pause button. Use the minimap and collect gifts for temporary power-ups.
